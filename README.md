## Explore the Wiki

This community-maintained wiki gathers practical information for players of the Eternal Starlight mod on Minecraft 1.21.1. It explains the always-dark Starlight dimension, including its biomes, generated landscapes, encounters, structures, and progression paths.

Use the reference to prepare journeys, locate points of interest, identify bosses and NPCs, and learn how the mod's growing range of equipment and machines works. Special topics include meteor showers, auroras, the Portal Ruins, the Gatekeeper, and the Orb of Prophecy.
